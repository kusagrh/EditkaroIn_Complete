Hi, I'm the person behind the cuts.

I run Editkaro.in, a video editing studio built around one idea — attention is earned in the first second, and kept one good cut at a time. What started as editing videos for friends turned into a full-time craft: I now work with creators, brands, and businesses to turn raw footage into content that actually performs.

My editing style leans cinematic but stays practical — every transition, sound cue, and color grade is there to serve the story, not to show off. Whether it's a 15-second reel or a 20-minute long-form video, the goal is the same: keep the viewer watching until the last frame.

Outside of the timeline, I'm constantly studying pacing, sound design, and color theory from films, ads, and the creators doing it best — because the craft never really stops evolving.
